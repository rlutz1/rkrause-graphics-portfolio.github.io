Hierarchical Modeling

The following canvas has two options: a basic "cake" structure and
(2) a starfish like creature with an animation playing over time.

You can play with the cake more with sliders to move all the individual
5 segments in order to illustrate the hierarchical modelling of the structure.
You can also turn on "Animation Mode" to watch it move with respect to time
(achieved with a rotation matrix given to each child in hierarchy).

You can also switch to the "starfish", which only moves with respect to time
in the same way as mentioned above. There are no sliders for this one because,
well, it would be a cluttered nightmare of sliders. So this one is just for fun.

You can find the vertex and fragment shaders used in vert_shaders.js and
frag_shaders.js. Most of the hierarchical modelling and stack building is
within scene_graph.js, where I begin fleshing out the beginning of a personal
API for buidling a scene graph. This will be evolving and tuned with time to make more
usable and flexible.
